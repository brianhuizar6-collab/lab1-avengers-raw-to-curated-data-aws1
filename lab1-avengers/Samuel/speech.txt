English

I owned Role 5: the serving and analytics layer. Once the ETL lands clean data in S3, my job is turning those files into tables people can query with SQL.

I set up Athena, created the curated and rejected databases and tables, and built the Glue job that registers new partitions — without that step the tables come back empty even though the data is there.

With that running, I executed the six analytical queries and confirmed the results matched what the ETL reported. Those results are the deliverable for requirement 9.
